## Problem

The README promises that, given a view and risk limits, the screener finds the structure that fits best. Nothing in the code does this yet. The measuring half (COLLECT through LEDG) is built; the choosing half is not.

## Intended behavior

Not yet defined beyond the README: candidates are ranked by fit to a stated view and risk limits, never by expected profit, and "rich" or "cheap" means against the rest of today's surface. The open questions are in A-004 of [../assumptions.md](../assumptions.md).

## Scope

To be set by the owner. Likely inputs, labeled **Proposed**: the newest snapshot's surface and term outputs, a view (for example a range, a direction, or a volatility stance over named sessions), and limits (max loss, max premium, max vega or gamma).
