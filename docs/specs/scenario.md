# SCEN: what a position does under hypothetical outcomes

## Purpose

Value, Greeks and P&L of a position under hypothetical spot levels, IV shifts, dates and paths, built on the snapshot's own IVs and term structure, each strike keeping its IV as spot moves, under three IV responses to the move. What-if only, never a forecast or a sort key. The UI and the structure chooser call it. Code: `screener/scenario.py` (built 2026-11-09). Tests: `tests/test_scenario.py`. Detail, acceptance criteria and verification evidence are in [../changes/2026-11-06-scenario-engine.md](../changes/2026-11-06-scenario-engine.md) and, for the spot rule and the IV response (2026-11-12), [../changes/2026-11-11-iv-response.md](../changes/2026-11-11-iv-response.md); the reasoning is in the module docstring.

## Interfaces

- `python app.py scenario <position.csv> [snapshot] [--shift N] [--sessions N[,N]]`, or `--path path.csv` instead of the last two; default the newest snapshot carried through `surface`. Not part of `daily`.
- Position CSV: `kind,expiry,strike,right,qty,price`; `kind` is `option`, `shares` or `nes`; `price` is the fill for hedge legs; on an option it is recorded as the fill (in `legs.csv` and the fingerprint) but not used, since entry is at mid. Path CSV: `date,spot,iv_shift,kind,qty,price`, the last three blank on a step with no trade.
- Writes `data/scenario/<snapshot>/<position>/legs.csv`, and `grid.csv` or `path.csv`, every row stamped with `inputs` (SCEN-014). Columns: `LEG_COLS`, `GRID_COLS`, `PATH_COLS` in `scenario.py`; meanings in [../concepts.md](../concepts.md) "What-if: the scenario engine".
- For the chooser and the UI: `scenario.load(snapshot)`, `build_legs`, `point(ctx, date, spot, shift, case)`, `price_point`, `grid(ctx, legs, shift, position, sessions)`, `grid_slope`, `extremes`, `response`, `run_path`; `CASES` names the responses. `Refused` carries every reason.
- Reads `data/chains/`, `data/iv/` and `data/surface/`, directly and through `term.load`, `context.session_remaining` and `ledger.snapshot_spot`. Time to expiry is `vol.years_to_expiry`, shared with `vol.one_expiry`.

## Gaps

- European math on American options (integrity.md item 2). The 0.9-delta flag is a placeholder until that item is measured. On real chains an in-the-money leg's model value sits up to $2-4 a share below its own mid; how much of that is early exercise rather than spread is not known.
- 1pm-close expiries are priced to 16:00 (A-003, deferred).
- The time roll inherits term.py's drop rule: on `2026-11-05_1633` only 3 of 15 expiries had a term total, so most legs kept a constant IV on later dates (A-015).
- Not checked against a broker's own scenario numbers.
- Which delta hedges SPY better, the fixed-IV delta or a response's slope, is untested (integrity.md item 5).
- Spreads priced outside their bounds are flagged `arb`, not repaired. With the slide, about 2-5% of 5-wide verticals and butterflies on the chains held broke their bounds after a spot move; with each strike's IV held, 7 of 146,880 verticals and 2.3% of butterflies, nearly all inside their quote bands (measurements.md 2026-11-11). A calendar's bound (one strike, two expiries) is not checked, and a response that differs by expiry could break one.
- The IV response is linear in the move. Past the moves it was sized on (+2.1% and -4.4%), a large rally takes IV to the floor: on `2026-11-10_1540`'s Nov-20 750 straddle, 10 of 52 grid points were flagged `iv_floor` (integrity.md item 6).
- The response is one shift per expiry; a change in skew is not modelled, and on Nov 5 the strike 5% above the old spot rose by half as much again as the old spot.
- The `sqrt(30/days)` scaling is provisional; net of the engine's own time roll, one of six measured moves did not follow it (integrity.md item 6).
- A-017 is open, deferred by the owner on 2026-11-13: its three readings stay as built for now.
