# SPY 764 long straddle, expiring 2026-09-25

First SIMULATED options trade, held to expiry on purpose: the point was to watch delta,
gamma, theta and vega evolve over a full month rather than to profit.

*NO DELTA HEDGING*

## The trade


| | |
| --- | --- |
| Entered | 2026-08-24, SPY at 763.66 |
| Structure | 2 calls + 2 puts, strike 764, expiring 2026-09-25 |
| Debit | 23.53 per straddle, $4,706.20 total |
| IV paid | 12.80% at the money |
| Break-evens | 740.47 and 787.53, so ±3.1% over 32 days |
| Daily bar | 0.806%, the move that pays for one day of theta |

The thesis, written at entry: *"unsure of direction, near ath, market quieted
down last week, potential bigger move on the way, giving it a month."* Bull
case, price moves into the 740 area; bear case, consolidation around the mid
780s.

## Outcome

SPY consolidated, closing at 771.35, ranging between 775.14 highest, 747.74 lowest.

Last line was unable to be recorded as puts expired worthless otm. Excel shows that
P&L as ($3,702) — parentheses are accounting format for negative, not a missing minus.

Settlement: calls finished 7.35 in the money, $1,470 back on $4,706.20 paid, so
-$3,236.20. Broker shows -$3,172; the $64 difference is a 4:15 exit above the 4:00
close, not fees.
